How to connect

01 Power on your drone.Wait for the kit's Wi-Fi network to appear.
02 Tap Connect kit Wi-Fi in this app.Choose this kit in Android's connection dialog. AP password: 12345678. Allow the requested Wi-Fi permission.
03 Stay in this app and check the connection.The app checks 192.168.4.1 after AP connects. For STA or Android 8–9, use Phone Wi-Fi settings and the kit address.

Join the kit Wi-Fi in phone settings, return to this app and check the connection.
