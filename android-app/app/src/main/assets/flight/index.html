How to connect

01 Power on your drone.For training, first configure the kit to join your router in STA mode.
02 Open your phone or laptop Wi-Fi settings.Real flight: join the kit AP with the password from the kit label. Training: join the same router as the STA kit and computer.
03 Return here and check the connection.Enter the kit’s router IP shown in the WebApp for training, then Check connection and pair. Enter its six-digit WebApp ID on app home.

Your browser cannot change Wi-Fi. Join the kit network in device settings first.
