V17.5.1 RESPONSIVE EMBED
----------------------
Use a full-width iframe/container. Do not lock the iframe to a desktop-only width.

Recommended desktop/tablet:
- width: 100%
- height: 900px or 90vh minimum

Recommended mobile:
- width: 100%
- height: 100vh / dynamic viewport height where Wix permits
- allow fullscreen
- avoid an additional horizontal-scroll wrapper around the iframe

The app itself detects phone/tablet/laptop/desktop/TV width and orientation automatically.
