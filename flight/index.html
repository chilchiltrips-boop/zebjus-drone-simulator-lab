How to connect

01 Power on your drone.Wait for the kit's Wi-Fi network to appear.
02 Open your phone or laptop Wi-Fi settings.Join this kit's unique network. Use the AP password from the kit label. Keep the connection even if it says “No internet”.
03 Return here and check the connection.Keep this app open and press Check connection. In STA mode, join the same router network as the kit.

Your browser cannot change Wi-Fi. Join the kit network in device settings first.
