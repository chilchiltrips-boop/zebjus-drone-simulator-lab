RATE HOLD TRAINING MODE:
- Stick active: Roll/Pitch commands angular rate.
- Stick release: capture current Roll/Pitch attitude.
- Disturbance: fixed attitude-capture helper creates a corrective rate target, then Rate PID controls the motors.

ANGLE MODE:
- Calibrate level stores Roll/Pitch reference.
- Stick release returns target to calibrated level.
- Angle PID -> Rate PID restores level after disturbance.
- Yaw remains Rate PID only.

TECHNICAL NOTE: Pure acro/rate mode normally holds rate only. V17.2 explicitly labels the requested behaviour as Rate Hold.
